## 1. Purpose and Scope

Quantum Flow Technologies is dedicated to providing a safe, professional, respectful, and collaborative environment for all contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and partners. 

This Code of Conduct outlines expectations for participant behavior across:
- The `quantum-flow-pqc-platform` code repository, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and code reviews.
- Project-related communications (electronic mail, messaging channels, design syncs, and architecture reviews).
- External technical representations of the project where an individual represents Quantum Flow Technologies.

---

## 2. Expected Behaviour

All participants are expected to:
- Demonstrate empathy, professional courtesy, and kindness toward other team members.
- Focus on what is best for the platform's security, engineering rigor, and long-term maintainability.
- Practice active listening and welcome diverse perspectives, backgrounds, and technical experience levels.
- Gracefully accept constructive criticism and address technical feedback objectively.
- Maintain rigorous standards of honesty regarding platform capabilities, test results, and known limitations.

---

## 3. Respectful and Inclusive Communication

- Use welcoming, respectful, and inclusive language at all times.
- Avoid derogatory, patronizing, or dismissive remarks in code reviews, commit messages, documentation, or design discussions.
- Be mindful of cultural differences, communication styles, and language proficiencies across our distributed engineering team.

---

## 4. Professional Collaboration

- Collaborate in good faith to resolve technical disagreements through empirical evidence, benchmark data, and architectural alignment.
- Adhere to the project's Definition of Done and agreed-upon sprint rhythms without circumventing review procedures.
- Help mentor and onboard fellow contributors, interns, and junior engineers constructively.

---

## 5. Constructive Technical Feedback

Code reviews and architecture debates must adhere to the highest standard of technical professionalism:
- Critique the code, architecture, or documentation—**never the person**.
- Clearly explain the rationale behind review requests, referencing specifications, security policies, or coding standards.
- Offer actionable alternatives rather than purely negative commentary.
- Acknowledge good work, clever optimizations, and clean documentation in peer reviews.

---

## 6. Respect for Intellectual Property

- Ensure all contributions consist of original work or properly licensed third-party code.
- Explicitly record library origins, licenses, and versions in accordance with our dependency governance rules.
- **Strictly prohibit** copying proprietary code, trade secrets, or unverified source material from third-party vendors or competitors.
- Maintain separation between company-owned intellectual property and open-source standards implementation.

---

## 7. Confidentiality and Customer Data Protection

- Protect customer metadata, scan targets, network topologies, and vulnerability findings as confidential business information.
- **Never insert real customer data, live credentials, or production environment artifacts into code, tests, or documentation.**
- Treat synthetic test environments and fixtures as the mandatory baseline for local and CI testing.

---

## 8. Responsible Handling of Security-Sensitive Information

As a cybersecurity platform inspecting cryptographic attack surfaces, contributors hold a special duty of care:
- **Never report security vulnerabilities or exploitable weaknesses via public GitHub issues, PRs, or public discussions.** Follow the private reporting protocols in [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).
- Exercise extreme caution when testing network discovery, TLS parsing, and socket interactions. Adhere strictly to deny-by-default scanning boundaries and written customer authorization.
- Never write or test exploit payloads intended to cause denial of service, resource exhaustion, or host compromise.

---

## 9. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited and constitute violations of this Code of Conduct:
- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or bullying of any individual based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or experience.
- Offensive, derogatory, or sexualized comments, humor, or imagery.
- Personal attacks, trolling, insulting remarks, or sustained disruption of technical discussions.
- Publishing or threatening to publish private personal information ("doxxing") or confidential credentials without explicit permission.
- Unauthorized scanning, probing, or testing of systems without written authorization.
- Circumventing security gates, tenant isolation checks, or automated linting/testing filters without documented approval.

## 11. Review and Enforcement Process

Upon receiving a report:
1. **Triage:** The designated review authority will acknowledge receipt within 2 business days.
2. **Investigation:** An objective review of the context, communications, and evidence will be conducted.
3. **Assessment:** The review authority will determine whether a violation occurred and assess its severity.
4. **Resolution:** Appropriate remedial or disciplinary action will be decided in alignment with company governance.

---

## 12. Consequences of Violations

Participants found to have violated this Code of Conduct may face tiered consequences based on severity:
- **Correction:** A private, written clarification and coaching regarding the violation and required behavioral correction.
- **Warning:** A formal written warning documenting the infraction and potential escalation paths.
- **Temporary Suspension:** Temporary suspension from repository access, code review responsibilities, or communication channels.
- **Permanent Ban & Termination:** Permanent revocation of repository privileges and escalation to corporate HR/legal leadership for contract or employment review.

---

## 13. Appeals and Review

Any participant subject to enforcement action may submit a written appeal within 14 calendar days of notification:
- Appeals must be directed to `[DESIGNATED COMPANY CONTACT — TO BE CONFIRMED: leadership-appeals@quantumflow.example.com]`.
- An independent review of the appeal and original findings will be conducted, and a final written determination will be issued.
